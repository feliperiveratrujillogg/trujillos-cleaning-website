Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Trujillo's Cleaning Services ("we," "us," or "our") operates trujilloscleaning.com. This page explains what information we collect through the site, how we use it, and who we share it with. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

When you submit the Request Service form, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the job (such as photos, notes, vehicle or room details). When you submit a job application, we collect your name, phone number, email, availability, work history details you choose to share, and your resume if you upload one.

We do not collect payment or financial information through this site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your service request and schedule an estimate or appointment
  • To review job applications and contact applicants
  • To improve our services and this website

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Contacting you

By submitting a form on this site, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message, or email about your request or application. Message and data rates may apply to text messages. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time by replying "STOP" to a text or telling us directly.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google Ads tracking (a small snippet of code from Google) to measure how many visitors submit a service request after clicking one of our ads. This works using cookies — small files stored in your browser. These cookies don't identify you personally; they help us understand whether our advertising is working.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings.

Third-party services

Form submissions on this site are processed by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, solely to deliver your submission to us by email. We may also communicate with you through Facebook Messenger or Instagram if you reach out to us there.

Data retention

We keep the information you submit for as long as needed to respond to your request or application, or as required by law.
