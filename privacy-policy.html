Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Trujillo's Cleaning Services LLC ("Trujillo's," "we," "us," or "our") operates trujilloscleaning.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have. By using this site or submitting a form, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

When you submit our Request Service form, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, mobile phone number, email address, service address, details about the job (such as vehicle, rooms, windows, areas, notes), and any photo you choose to upload. When you submit a job application, we collect your name, phone number, email, availability, work experience you choose to share, and your resume if you upload one.

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as your browser type, pages visited, and the page you came from, using cookies and similar technologies (see "Cookies and tracking" below). We do not collect payment card or bank account information through this website.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your service request, send you a quote, and schedule your appointment
  • To contact you by phone, text message (if you opted in), or email about your request, appointment, or application
  • To review job applications
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our business

SMS / text messaging

Our Request Service form has two separate, optional text message consent boxes, both unchecked by default. If you check the Service-Related SMS Consent box, you agree to receive non-marketing text messages from Trujillo's Cleaning Services LLC about your quotes, scheduling, appointment reminders, and service updates. If you check the Marketing SMS Consent box, you agree to receive marketing and promotional text messages, including special offers, discounts, and promotions. You can choose either, both, or neither. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not required to purchase services, and we record each choice separately with your submission.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message. For help, reply HELP or contact us using the details below. After you opt out we will send one confirmation message and then stop texting you. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We may use trusted service providers (for example, our messaging and customer-management software) only to deliver messages and operate our business on our behalf, and they may not use your information for their own marketing.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (such as form processing, messaging, scheduling, and hosting providers), when required by law, or to protect our rights and safety. As stated above, we do not share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses cookies and similar technologies, including Google Ads tracking, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure whether our ads lead to service requests. Advertising and analytics cookies stay off until you accept them in our cookie notice. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser, control cookies through your browser settings, or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access limited to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We keep the information you submit for as long as needed to respond to your request or application, provide our services, and meet legal and business record-keeping needs.

Your rights and choices

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, to stop texting you (reply STOP), or to stop emailing or calling you. To make a request, contact us below and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also decline cookies using our cookie notice.

Children's privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Our text messaging program is for people 18 and older.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. See also our Terms of Service.
